## In-text annotations

 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Subsection (b) (1)'s prohibition on using "captur[ed]" data in a disloyal manner prevents an undercover employee from publishing a critical article based on any notes she takes of documents or policies laid out in a breakroom.</mark>  ([Page 8](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/WY7PRV7E?page=8&annotation=A7LPBJJB)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Subsection (b)(2) forbids including a photograph of the same documents</mark>  ([Page 8](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/WY7PRV7E?page=8&annotation=BXU8EP27)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Subsection (b)(3) then punishes the undercover employee for placing an unattended camera on the factory floor while she works</mark>  ([Page 8](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/WY7PRV7E?page=8&annotation=HNPB9LFK)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">And the "catch-all" (b)(5), Opening Br. 8, may reach even mere reporting of a conversation had with other employees—to a newspaper, a union, a state agency—if the reporting leads the State to shut down the facility.</mark>  ([Page 8](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/WY7PRV7E?page=8&annotation=TMYPYLYE))

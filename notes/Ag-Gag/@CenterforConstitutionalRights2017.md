 <mark class="hltr-yellow">In recent years, these measures have expanded beyond the agricultural industry to target whistleblowers more broadly. In 2017, the governor of Arkansas signed into law an expansive ag-gag bill that aims to silence whistleblowers in agriculture, child care, and nursing facilities.</mark>  ([Page 5](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/NEX5N6G5?page=5&annotation=highlight-p7x56y448)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Te term “ag-gag” was coined in 2011 by former New York Times columnist Mark Bittman to describe a series of state bills appearing across the country that criminalized photographing and video recording inside agricultural facilities.</mark>  ([Page 6](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/NEX5N6G5?page=6&annotation=highlight-p8x56y643))

## In-text annotations

 <mark class="hltr-yellow">367 NLRB No. 112</mark>  ([Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x64y64)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Contrary to the judge, we find that the record evidence does not show that this brief conversation involved</mark>  ([Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=1&annotation=highlight-p1x312y163)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">protected concerted activity. Laff’s credited testimony, objectively viewed, shows that the brief conversation focused only on a personal complaint by Woods about receiving the customer call and that neither he nor Laff contemplated taking any concerted action about this event that would be for improvement of their working conditions or those of fellow employees.</mark>  ([Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=2&annotation=highlight-p2x63y617)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">concerted activity includes cases “‘where individual employees seek to initiate or to induce or to prepare for group action, as well as individual employees bringing truly group complaints to the attention of management.’” Meyers Industries, 281 NLRB 882, 887 (1986) (Meyers II),</mark>  ([Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=2&annotation=highlight-p2x63y235)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">activity may still be concerted even if it involves: “only a speaker and a listener, but to qualify as such, it must appear at the very least it was engaged in with the object of initiating or inducing or preparing for group action or that it had some relation to group action in the interest of employees. . . . Activity which consists of mere talk must, in order to be protected, be talk looking toward group action . . . . [I]f it looks forward to no action at all, it is more than likely to be mere ‘griping.’” Mushroom Transportation Co. v. NLRB, 330 F.2d 683, 685 (3d Cir. 1964).</mark>  ([Page 2](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=2&annotation=highlight-p2x312y533)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">But even if the conversation qualifies as concerted action between Woods and Laff with respect to the routing of customer calls, we find that it was not for a goal of “mutual aid or protection” and, therefore, would still be unprotected.</mark>  ([Page 3](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/9ZY5WNP3?page=3&annotation=highlight-p3x63y502))
